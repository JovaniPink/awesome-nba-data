# Content and code licensing

This repository shares a public catalog for NBA data and analysis. The licensing policy
below takes effect with the commit that introduces it; the dated rights review is a
record of preparation, not a claim that an unmerged proposal is already published.

| Material | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| Original catalog content and compilation: README, original documentation, catalog descriptions and generated projections, and contribution-template prose | CC BY 4.0, in [licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt](licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt), subject to the retained notices below |
| Supporting code: scripts, tests and fixtures, workflow automation, executable configuration, and code examples | MIT, in [LICENSE-CODE](LICENSE-CODE) |
| Earlier MIT contributions and copies of previously MIT-published material | Existing MIT rights and notice remain in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) |
| Third-party material, including the adapted code of conduct and external resources | Its own stated terms and attribution; see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) |

CC BY 4.0 permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial reuse, with appropriate
credit, a license link, and an indication of changes. It does not require permission
for uses that copyright does not restrict. Our catalog license does not relicense the
data, APIs, articles, or software that the catalog links to. The generated index's
`source.license` fields describe external-source boundaries, not this catalog's license.

## Reuse and attribution

A suggested attribution is:

> Adapted from awesome-nba-data, maintained by Jovani Pink and community contributors,
> with support from The Measured Studios. Source: https://github.com/JovaniPink/awesome-nba-data.
> Content licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
> Changes: describe your modifications.

Use an accurate change statement. Retain applicable attribution and notices from
[NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md), including the MIT notice when copying earlier MIT material.
The example is a convenient attribution format, not an extra condition beyond the
applicable licenses. No trademark rights or endorsement by Jovani Pink, The Measured
Studios, contributors, or data publishers are granted by this notice.

## Earlier releases and contributors

The new policy does not withdraw earlier MIT permissions or public-domain dedications.
New original content and compilation contributions are offered under CC BY 4.0;
pre-existing third-party contributions retain their recorded licenses. We do not claim
exclusive CC BY rights over the entire historical catalog or an assignment of contributor
ownership. A recipient may continue using earlier material under its earlier grant.

The [October 20 rights review](docs/license-review-2026-10-20.md) identifies the inspected
history and submissions. No private contributor agreement or separate relicensing
consent was represented as obtained. This preserves the grants evidenced by the public
record instead of assuming consent from a merge or thank-you comment.

## Future contributions

By submitting original material for inclusion, you agree to license your content under
CC BY 4.0 and your code under MIT, according to the scope above. You retain ownership.
Submit only material you may license on those terms; identify copied/adapted material
and its attribution or permission. Flag any different terms before inclusion so the
maintainer can review them. A resource suggestion does not transfer rights in the
suggested resource. See [contributing.md](contributing.md) for the submission process.
